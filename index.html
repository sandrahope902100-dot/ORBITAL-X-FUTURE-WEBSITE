ORBITAL X FUTURE is an independent technology venture founded by Jamaican entrepreneur Sasha-Lee Nugent. The venture focuses on artificial intelligence, software development and emerging digital experiences, with a mission to turn ambitious ideas into practical tools that help people and businesses move forward.

02 / MEET THE FOUNDER

Sasha-Lee
Nugent.

Founder & Technology Entrepreneur

Sasha-Lee Nugent is a Jamaican entrepreneur and the founder of ORBITAL X FUTURE. Her professional background includes Business Administration – Level 2 training, a Diploma in Patient Care Assistance, BPO customer service, inside sales and AI-powered content creation.

She is building the venture through hands-on product development and practical exploration of artificial intelligence. Her work on Sasha AI has involved React, JavaScript, AI API integration, GitHub repository management and application deployment.
