PRE-LAUNCH DOCUMENTATION

Policies & service terms.

Important status notice

ORBITAL X FUTURE is an independent technology venture, not an incorporated or registered company. The directory is not live, no listings are currently being published, and no payments are accepted. This page describes the proposed rules for a future service; it is not an offer to sell or a substitute for a signed order. Final terms must be reviewed for the markets served before launch.

Last reviewed: 27 November 2026

1. Directory status and eligibility

The directory is in preparation. An enquiry does not create a listing, reserve space, establish a customer relationship, or guarantee publication. A business may be asked to confirm that it is authorised to submit its details. The current application form does not accept file or image uploads; any supporting material would be requested separately if needed. ORBITAL X FUTURE may decline, correct, suspend or remove material that is inaccurate, unlawful, misleading, unsafe, duplicated, out of date or inconsistent with published directory standards.

2. Standard Listing — proposed free term

  • A Standard Listing is proposed to run for 12 months from its publication date.
  • It may be renewed for another 12 months at no charge after the business confirms its details remain accurate and the listing still meets the directory rules.
  • Free inclusion does not guarantee traffic, enquiries, leads, sales, search position or endorsement.
  • A listing may be corrected, suspended or removed if the business closes, asks for removal, cannot confirm material details, or breaches the directory rules.
  • There is no automatic paid conversion. A free listing will never become a paid plan without the business's explicit written agreement.

3. Proposed paid plans and advertising

The prices on the main website are proposed introductory rates only: Business Plus US$9.99/month, Featured Business US$19.99/month and Premium Partner US$39.99/month. They are not currently purchasable. Before any paid service launches, the business must receive a written order describing the exact deliverables, placement, campaign dates, creative, price, billing currency, applicable taxes or fees, reporting and contact for support.

  • Business Plus is proposed as an enhanced profile service, not a ranking boost.
  • Featured Business and Premium Partner are proposed advertising packages with defined campaign periods and visible “Sponsored” or “Advertisement” labels.
  • Paid placement does not buy verification, a favourable review, an editorial recommendation, or a guaranteed position in ordinary results.
  • Proposed billing is month-to-month with no minimum commitment. Any cancellation method, effective date, renewal, refund and make-good terms will be stated in the written order before payment is enabled.
  • No payment is due now. Do not send card details, passwords, identity documents or payment to an individual account.

4. Accuracy, claims and third-party businesses

Businesses are responsible for the accuracy and lawful use of the information, logos, photographs and claims they submit. A directory entry is not an audit, certification, licence check, recommendation or guarantee of service quality. Visitors should independently verify credentials, prices, availability, insurance, reviews and contractual terms directly with the business.

5. Advertising transparency

Any paid placement will be clearly identified beside the advertisement. Sponsorship must not be presented as an independent ranking or recommendation. Unpaid listings will not be downgraded merely because a business does not purchase advertising. Any future ranking or sorting method will be described accurately.

6. Enquiries and communications

The business application form and founder-only review interface are connected to the Supabase database. The database setup and core approval workflow have been tested using temporary synthetic records; those test records were rolled back. A separate live browser and Android mobile test of the public form remains outstanding. No business listings are currently published. There is no business self-service account, automated booking, payment checkout or lead-management system. Email links remain available for general enquiries.

7. Privacy notice

The directory form collects business name, category, location, description, optional public website/email/phone, applicant name and private contact email, plus separate confirmations of authority, privacy consent and permission to publish the business profile if approved. Applications are stored in the Supabase project and remain private while pending. Only the verified founder account can review them. If approved, only business profile fields and selected public contact details are published; the applicant’s private contact name and email are not published. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, medical information or other sensitive information.

The website is hosted by Render, uses Supabase for authentication and directory data, loads the Supabase browser SDK from the esm.sh CDN, and loads web fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical connection information under their own privacy terms. The site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Basic anti-abuse measures include a hidden form honeypot and a limit of three submissions per contact email in a rolling 24-hour period; these measures do not prevent all spam or abuse. A fixed retention period for application records has not yet been established; applicants can request correction or deletion by contacting us, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

For a request to correct or delete an enquiry you sent, email orbitalxfuture@gmail.com and identify the message or business enquiry. We will assess the request and any legal or operational retention requirements. Do not assume an email deletion request automatically removes copies held by your email provider or other recipients.

8. Security and availability

We take reasonable steps appropriate to this pre-launch website, but no internet service can be guaranteed secure, uninterrupted or error-free. Do not submit confidential material through ordinary email. The directory and any future paid features may change before launch.

9. Changes, governing law and legal review

These proposed rules may be revised before launch. The final customer agreement must identify the service provider's legal identity, contact address, governing law, dispute process, tax treatment and any mandatory consumer rights applicable to the customer and market. Those items have not yet been finalised, so no governing-law or jurisdiction promise is made here. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
