PRE-LAUNCH DOCUMENTATION

Policies & service terms.

Important status notice

ORBITAL X FUTURE is an independent technology venture, not an incorporated or registered company. The directory subscription and manual payment workflow are being prepared. Public applications remain closed during final testing. No listings are currently being published and no payments are accepted. This page describes proposed service rules; it is not an offer to sell or a substitute for a final customer agreement.

Last reviewed: 28 November 2026

1. Directory status and eligibility

The directory is in preparation. An enquiry does not create a listing, reserve space, establish a customer relationship, or guarantee publication. A business may be asked to confirm that it is authorised to submit its details. The current application form does not accept file or image uploads; any supporting material would be requested separately if needed. ORBITAL X FUTURE may decline, correct, suspend or remove material that is inaccurate, unlawful, misleading, unsafe, duplicated, out of date or inconsistent with published directory standards.

2. Monthly directory subscriptions

All directory packages are monthly prepaid subscriptions. Jamaican businesses are quoted and billed in Jamaican dollars (JMD). Businesses outside Jamaica are quoted and billed in United States dollars (USD). The country supplied in the application determines the currency and package amount.

  • Standard: J$1,999/month in Jamaica or US$9.99/month internationally. Includes one standard business profile and ordinary directory search placement.
  • Premium: J$3,999/month in Jamaica or US$19.99/month internationally. Includes Standard benefits plus sponsored placement above Standard listings in directory search results, with a visible Sponsored label.
  • Featured: J$6,999/month in Jamaica or US$39.99/month internationally. Includes Premium benefits plus top-priority sponsored placement in directory search results, with a visible Featured · Sponsored label.
  • All applications are reviewed before acceptance. Approval alone does not publish a listing.
  • After approval, payment instructions are sent by email. The business must pay the stated amount and include its application or listing reference.
  • Payment is verified manually by the founder against the receiving bank or payment account. A screenshot or receipt supplied by the customer is not, by itself, proof that funds have cleared.
  • A listing remains unpublished until the full payment is independently confirmed. The one-month subscription period begins when the listing is activated.
  • There is no automatic card charge or automatic renewal. The business must make a new monthly payment to continue the service. A listing is removed from public search when its paid period expires unless renewal payment is confirmed.
  • Cancellation requests may be sent by email before the next monthly payment. No further monthly payment is due unless the business requests renewal.
  • Paid placement does not buy verification, a favourable review, editorial endorsement or guaranteed customer results.

3. Manual payment, cancellation and refunds

Payment instructions are provided individually by email after application approval. Payment methods and receiving details will be shown in the payment email. Do not send passwords, card security codes or online banking credentials. The founder records the payment reference and confirmation date; no listing is published before funds are independently verified.

The final refund and dispute process, the service provider's legal identity and address, applicable tax treatment, and market-specific consumer terms are still being finalized. No payment will be requested or accepted until these items are included in the final customer agreement. Any mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

4. Accuracy, claims and third-party businesses

Businesses are responsible for the accuracy and lawful use of the information, logos, photographs and claims they submit. A directory entry is not an audit, certification, licence check, recommendation or guarantee of service quality. Visitors should independently verify credentials, prices, availability, insurance, reviews and contractual terms directly with the business.

5. Advertising transparency

Any paid placement will be clearly identified beside the advertisement. Sponsorship must not be presented as an independent ranking or recommendation. Unpaid listings will not be downgraded merely because a business does not purchase advertising. Any future ranking or sorting method will be described accurately.

6. Enquiries and communications

The business application form, founder review interface and manual payment workflow are connected to Supabase. Applications remain closed while the complete workflow is tested in a live browser and on Android. The founder dashboard can store separate JMD and USD payment instructions, draft payment emails, mark instructions as sent, record a verified payment reference and activate a one-month listing only after confirmation. Monthly renewals also require a new payment and manual confirmation. No business self-service account, automated card billing, payment checkout or lead-management system is provided.

7. Privacy notice

The directory form collects business name, category, country and city, description, optional public website/email/phone, applicant name and private contact email, selected monthly package, and separate confirmations of authority, privacy consent, subscription terms and permission to publish the business profile if approved. For approved applications, the system also stores the assigned currency and monthly amount, payment status, payment reference and payment/activation dates. Founder payment instructions are stored privately in the database and are accessible only to the verified founder. Applications are stored in the Supabase project and remain private while pending. Only the verified founder account can review them. If approved, only business profile fields and selected public contact details are published; the applicant’s private contact name and email are not published. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, medical information or other sensitive information.

The website is hosted by Render, uses Supabase for authentication and directory data, loads the Supabase browser SDK from the esm.sh CDN, and loads web fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical connection information under their own privacy terms. The site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Basic anti-abuse measures include a hidden form honeypot and a limit of three submissions per contact email in a rolling 24-hour period; these measures do not prevent all spam or abuse. A fixed retention period for application and report records has not yet been established; applicants and reporters can request correction or deletion by contacting us, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Public listing correction and safety reports are stored privately and visible only to the founder for review. Reporter contact email is not published.

For a request to correct or delete an enquiry you sent, email orbitalxfuture@gmail.com and identify the message or business enquiry. We will assess the request and any legal or operational retention requirements. Do not assume an email deletion request automatically removes copies held by your email provider or other recipients.

8. Security and availability

We take reasonable steps appropriate to this pre-launch website, but no internet service can be guaranteed secure, uninterrupted or error-free. Do not submit confidential material through ordinary email. The directory and any future paid features may change before launch.

9. Changes, governing law and legal review

These proposed rules may be revised before launch. The final customer agreement must identify the service provider's legal identity, contact address, governing law, dispute process, tax treatment and any mandatory consumer rights applicable to the customer and market. Those items have not yet been finalised, so no governing-law or jurisdiction promise is made here. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
